Open Access Publishing

Augmented Humans 2027 is sponsored by ACM SIGCHI, and all accepted papers are published Open Access in the ACM Digital Library.

There is no Article Processing Charge (APC) for most authors:

  • Free if the corresponding author's institution participates in the ACM Open program.
  • Free if the corresponding author's institution is in a country covered by an ACM waiver.
  • 50% discount for corresponding authors based in Sri Lanka.

Eligibility is determined by the corresponding author's institution, so it is worth checking this before you submit. See ACM's author guidance for the full policy.

Choosing your Corresponding Author

ACM sends all e-rights and TAPS correspondence to the Corresponding Author only — no one else on the paper is copied. Two things follow from this:

  • If any author's institution is in ACM Open, list that person as the Corresponding Author in PCS. The fee is set by their institution.
  • Make sure the Corresponding Author will be reachable and able to act during the camera-ready period.

Completing the e-rights form and the TAPS submission by the camera-ready deadline, February 5th, 2027 (AoE), is mandatory. A paper whose e-rights and TAPS steps are not completed in time cannot be published in the proceedings.

For questions about publication, e-rights or TAPS, contact the Publication Co-Chairs at proceedings-ahs27@acm.org.

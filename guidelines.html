Submission

Augmented Humans 2027 uses a streamlined structure for technical program submissions. Submissions fall into the following categories: Full & Short Papers, Posters & Demos, and Workshops. Proposals to host a future edition of Augmented Humans are also welcome. Further guidance for authors and organizers will be provided as the conference approaches.

Deadlines and notifications for every track are listed on the homepage.

Overview

This hub links to all guidelines for Augmented Humans 2027. Use the quick links below to find track-specific formats, templates, deadlines, and presentation requirements.

Deadlines for Posters & Demos and for Workshops are still to be announced. Those pages set out the format and requirements in full, and the dates will be added as they are confirmed.

Submission Guidelines

The general instructions that apply to every track: submission categories, the full deadline table, how to use the ACM Primary Article Template, the TAPS production workflow, and ACM ICPS Open Access publishing information.

Visit: Submission Guidelines

Full & Short Papers

Track-specific requirements for Full (up to 16 pages) and Short (up to 8 pages) papers in single-column format for review, anonymization policy, and direct links to ACM Word/LaTeX templates and Overleaf. This page also lists Full/Short paper deadlines.

Visit: Full & Short Papers

Posters & Demos

Combined guidelines for Posters and Demos, including aims & scope, the three presentation styles (poster-only, poster + desk, poster + demo booth), submission format, evaluation criteria, and what to prepare for hands-on presentations. Deadlines are TBA.

Visit: Posters & Demos

Workshops

How to propose a workshop for Augmented Humans 2027: what a proposal must cover, how proposals are reviewed, and how accepted workshops are published. Deadlines are TBA. For questions, email the Workshop Co-Chairs at workshops-ahs27@acm.org.

Visit: Workshops
